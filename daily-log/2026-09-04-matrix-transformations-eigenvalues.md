## Topics Covered

- **Rotation matrices** — cosine/sine formula and counter-clockwise convention. ✅
- **Scaling matrices** — stretching/compressing along axes. ✅
- **Shearing matrices** — tilting space into parallelograms. ✅
- **Reflection matrices** — flipping across an axis. ✅
- **Composition** — multiplying transformations; order matters. ✅
- **Eigenvectors** — directions that survive a transformation. ✅
- **Eigenvalues** — scaling factors associated with eigenvectors. ✅
- **Eigendecomposition** — `A = V @ D @ V⁻¹`. ✅
- **PCA connection** — eigenvectors of the covariance matrix. ✅
- **RNN stability** — eigenvalues greater than 1 can lead to exploding gradients; less than 1 can lead to vanishing gradients. ✅
- **Covariance matrix** — `C = (1/n) × Xᵀ @ X`. ✅
